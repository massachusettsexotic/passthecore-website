Pass the Core Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Pass the Core is a study app for the U.S. CORE pesticide applicator certification exam. This policy explains what the app does (and does not do) with information on your device.

TL;DR

  • Pass the Core does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information about you.
  • The app makes no network requests of any kind — the entire question bank is bundled with the app and everything works offline.
  • Pass the Core does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party trackers.
  • Everything you do in the app — chosen state, language preference, test progress, and results — is stored only on your device.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following in your iPhone's local app storage. None of it is transmitted anywhere.

  • Your chosen state. Used to select the matching state-law question section.
  • Language preference. English, Spanish, or match-the-phone.
  • Test sessions and results. In-progress tests, completed tests, your answers, and score statistics.

Deleting the app permanently deletes all of this data.

Information collected

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no network access.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes any of the above, this policy will be updated at this URL and the "Last updated" date revised before that version is released.
