Practice questions for the pesticide applicator CORE exam, written against the material the exam is actually drawn from — label law, personal protective equipment, calibration, drift, storage and disposal, environmental protection, and the maths. Every answer comes with the explanation, so a wrong one teaches something instead of just costing a point.

All fifty states

The CORE material is broadly common, but the licensing is not. The app carries a direct link to the licensing authority for every one of the fifty states, so you can find the real application, the real fee and the real renewal schedule without guessing.

In English and Spanish

Every question is written in both. A large share of the people who need this licence work in Spanish, and study material for them is thin.

Works offline

The whole question bank is on the phone. Study in a truck, in a barn, on a job site — no signal needed. Export your results as a PDF when you want a record of where you stand.
